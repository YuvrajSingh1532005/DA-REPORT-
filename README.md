🥛 Amul Product Sales Performance Analysis (2019–2025)

📌 Project Overview

This project presents a Descriptive Analytics Report on Amul Product Sales Performance from 2019 to 2025. It analyzes product sales volume, regional demand, customer ratings, revenue, profitability, pricing differences, and sales trends to identify important business insights.

The project uses data analytics techniques to understand historical sales performance and support data-driven business decisions.

Note: The dataset used in this project is synthetic and designed to represent realistic FMCG dairy industry patterns. It does not contain Amul's actual confidential transaction data.

🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Identify the best-selling Amul products by sales volume.
- Analyze sales trends from 2019 to 2025.
- Compare product demand across four Indian regions.
- Evaluate customer ratings across products and years.
- Identify the highest-revenue-generating products.
- Analyze regional profitability per unit sold.
- Compare average retail prices and ex-factory prices across regions.
- Identify the most important product-region revenue combinations.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

📊 Project Highlights

- Time Period: 2019–2025
- Dataset Size: Approximately 300,000 simulated units
- Product Categories: 20
- Regions Covered: 4
- Total Revenue: Approximately ₹3.03 crore
- Average Product Rating: 4.13/5

🛠️ Tools and Technologies

Insight: Product ratings remain relatively stable, with only small differences between the highest- and lowest-rated products.

The project compares average retail prices and the gap between retail and ex-factory prices.

🔍 Key Findings

1. Sales leadership: Amul Dahi leads in unit sales, while Amul Ghee leads in total revenue.
2. Sales growth: Total annual sales volume increased substantially from 2019 to 2025, with a temporary decline in 2020.
3. Regional demand: West India consistently ranks first in total sales volume.
4. Profitability: North India records the highest average profit per unit.
5. Customer satisfaction: The overall average product rating is approximately 4.13/5.
6. Revenue concentration: Amul Ghee in West India is the strongest product-region revenue combination.
7. Pricing differences: East India has the largest gap between retail and ex-factory prices.

💡 Business Recommendations

- Improve the profitability of West India by reviewing discounts, pricing, and product mix.
- Maintain product availability and distribution efficiency for Amul Ghee in West India.
- Monitor customer ratings regularly to identify potential changes in satisfaction.
- Study North India's pricing and product mix to understand its higher profit per unit.
- Investigate the comparatively lower rating of Amul Gold Milk.
- Review regional distribution and logistics costs, particularly in East India.
- Explore predictive analytics to forecast future sales, revenue, and regional demand.
